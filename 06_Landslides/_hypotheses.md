Hypotheses (not for the report)
 
Landslides in steep terrain that release disrupted material have the potential for long runout from bouncing, rolling rocks. A broader grain size distribution may result in a runout distance consistent with a discrete failure independent of ground motion. However, because of the doublet effect in the June 24, 2026 events, it's possible that additional ground shaking from the second event caused runouts from failures triggered by the first event to extend further than they would have otherwise. It is difficult to anticipate how this effect might manifest itself in the field. One way of detecting this effect would be to characterize the runout length of mapped landslides and determine wthether the data is a better match to a normal or a bi-normal distribution. The latter would indicate different discrete conditions (e.g., still ground surface during runout or dynamic ground surface during runout?).
